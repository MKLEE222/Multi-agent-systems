# OACR COMPOSE — Mechanism Contrast Freeze

**Date:** 2026-09-30  
**Status:** working hypothesis frozen while learned recovery-hysteresis outcomes are still unobserved  
**Purpose:** prevent post-hoc interpretation of sequence-depth results

## 1. Rejected generic hypothesis

Do not use the claim:

> longer operation sequences naturally expose more hidden state.

Existing evidence already rejects this as a generic explanation:

- relational deletion R3/R4: distinctions are depth-1 active or inert under the entire frozen action universe;
- GRACE L1b: 224 H0 collisions remain equivalent through the complete registered H2 panel.

Therefore sequence length alone is not the target mechanism.

## 2. Working mechanism hypothesis

Let \(\Gamma_X\) denote the continuation interface at state \(X\).

The working hypothesis is:

> delayed representational demand becomes scientifically plausible when an earlier transformation changes the continuation interface governing later transformations.

Schematically:

\[
(X,\Gamma_X)
\xrightarrow{a_1}
(X',\Gamma_{X'})
\]

with

\[
\Gamma_{X'}\ne\Gamma_X
\]

in a carrier-relevant coordinate such as:

- legality / qualification;
- enabledness / materializability;
- recoverability;
- later-write response geometry;
- future action outcome semantics.

The claim is **not** that every such change produces delayed divergence.

It is a mechanism condition that distinguishes meaningful COMPOSE from merely applying more fixed-domain operations.

## 4. Falsification discipline

The mechanism hypothesis must be weakened if:

- learned recovery produces abundant exact current recovery but no future-response difference under a well-powered frozen panel;
- another fixed-domain carrier yields strong delayed divergence without any carrier-relevant change in continuation interface;
- apparent positives reduce to current-state mismatch, current legality mismatch, or post-hoc action selection.

## 5. Claim boundary

Even if supported, do not claim:

> state-dependent qualification is necessary for all composition effects.

A safer eventual claim would be:

> Across the audited carriers, new representational demand did not arise merely from increasing action depth; the clean positive mechanism observed so far arises when transformations change the semantics or qualification of later continuation.

This remains an empirical pattern until broader carriers are tested.
